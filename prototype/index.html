Perde — Navigable prototype

Screen sketches · Event discovery & ticketing platform · November 3, 2026

Each screen is shown both as a wireframe sketch and as the final design of the working prototype. Click a screen to open it, then use the highlighted areas to move through the app the way a user would. Clicking anywhere outside them flashes all clickable areas. Use the arrow keys to step through the screens in order, and press W to switch between wireframe and final design.

Note: the final design is localised in Turkish for its target market; the wireframes and all annotations are in English.

Main user flow: Welcome → Sign in → Survey → Explore → Event page → Choose tickets → Seat map (3D view) → Checkout → Tickets are ready → My tickets.
Also: Search, Category & filters, Best sellers, Reviews, Suflör AI assistant, Mobile.
